    The .pkl files are NOT directly loadable on Android.

          (matching the existing DecisionTree.kt pattern in the app), OR
      (c) Call a hosted REST API serving the model.
    The exported feature_importances.csv and classification metrics are
    immediately usable in a research paper without any Android changes.
